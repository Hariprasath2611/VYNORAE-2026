# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Students, developers, tech enthusiasts, and innovators participating in VYNORAE 2026, a national hackathon on emerging technologies.

## Product Purpose
VYNORAE 2026 landing page serves to excite, inform, and convert visitors into registered hackathon teams by highlighting prize pools, themes, sponsors, event coordinators, and FAQs.

## Brand Personality
Futuristic, High-Tech, Cyberpunk, High Energy, Cutting-Edge.

## Anti-references
Generic corporate templates, dull white/gray backgrounds, slow corporate fluff, uninspired traditional conference layouts.

## Design Principles
1. Immersive High-Tech Aesthetics: Evoke the feeling of entering a cyber-hud or next-gen technological control center.
2. Clear Conversion Pathways: Keep the registration CTA prominent, accessible, and high contrast.
3. Rhythm & Visual Impact: Use dynamic typography, neon accents, and sharp contrasts without overwhelming readability.
4. Mobile-First & Performant: Ensure animations and videos load smoothly across all viewports without layout shifts or thrashing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Targeting WCAG AA compliance with clear contrast ratios, legible typography, proper keyboard navigation, and alternative media attributes for all attendees.
